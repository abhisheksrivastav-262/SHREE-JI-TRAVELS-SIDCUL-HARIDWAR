Four Sacred Destinations

The Char Dhams

Separate journeys, one sacred circuit. Descriptions are brief introductions — darshan order and halts are planned with you.

Yamunotri

Source of the Yamuna, reached via road and trek. Discuss fitness, halts and timing with us.

Gangotri

Source of the Ganga amid Himalayan valleys. Road access and halts confirmed per season.

Kedarnath

One of the holiest Shiva shrines, with road + trek/transport stages. Conditions vary by season.

Badrinath

Sacred Vishnu shrine in the high Himalayas. Opening dates and stays confirmed in advance.

How a Yatra Plan Is Discussed

This is an illustrative itinerary framework, not a confirmed package. We discuss a practical order with you:

  • 1Haridwar → Yamunotri region (road + local stages)
  • 2Yamunotri region → Gangotri region
  • 3Gangotri region → Kedarnath region
  • 4Kedarnath region → Badrinath → Haridwar

Halts, darshan time, vehicle choice and duration are fixed only after direct confirmation. No package prices, hotel inclusions, permits or guaranteed darshan are offered online.
